Five systems, one intelligence layer.

Each module solves a distinct failure in how welfare is delivered today — from citizen-facing detection to government-facing governance tools.

Module

Missed Benefits Detector

Citizens enter age, income, occupation, state, and education. The engine returns eligible schemes, estimated benefit value, required documents, application links — and critically, schemes they're likely eligible for but not yet claiming.

Eligibility inference
Module

Scheme Leakage & Fraud Detection

Flags duplicate beneficiaries, suspicious application clusters, unusual approval patterns, and ghost beneficiaries — giving departments a governance and audit layer, not just a delivery tool.

Anomaly detection
Module

Scheme Impact Heatmap

Overlays beneficiary coverage against economic, education, and employment indicators on a map — turning "how many received it" into "is it actually working."

Module

Application Failure Predictor

Before submission, checks for missing documents and eligibility gaps, surfaces likely rejection reasons, and returns a success probability score — cutting rejection rates before they happen.

The Unique Twist

Give every citizen a privacy-protected welfare twin.

Instead of a static eligibility check, every citizen gets a continuously-updated profile that tracks benefits already received, entitlements still missing, and eligibility that opens up as their life changes — a birthday, a new address, a completed degree.
