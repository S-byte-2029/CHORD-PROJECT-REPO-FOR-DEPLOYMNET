About CHORD

Connecting citizens to the benefits they deserve.

CHORD is designed to make government schemes easier to discover, understand and access — all from one place.

The problem

Finding the right scheme shouldn't be difficult.

Government welfare schemes can provide valuable support, but people may not know which schemes are available to them or whether they are eligible.

Information can also be spread across different portals, making it difficult to understand eligibility requirements, required documents and application processes.

What CHORD does

One simpler way to discover schemes.

CHORD brings important scheme information together and presents it in a simpler and more user-friendly way.

02 / ELIGIBILITY

Know what you need

04 / BENEFITS

Personalized discovery
